   194→   194→    |
   195→   195→    +-- WebCrypto / mature cryptographic library
   196→   196→    |
   197→   197→    +-- WebRTC DataChannel
   198→   198→    |
   199→   199→    +-- Minimal signaling / rendezvous mechanism
   200→   200→    |
   201→   201→    +-- Optional STUN/TURN infrastructure
   202→   202→    |

   206→   206→
   207→   207→WebRTC requires signaling.
   208→   208→
   209→   209→Therefore do NOT falsely advertise:
   210→   210→
   211→   211→"100% backend-free"
